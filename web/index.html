THE FIELD GUIDE

From “why is it slow?”
to “there it is.”

  1. 01

    Capture your network

    In Chrome, Edge or Firefox, open Developer Tools → Network. Reload your page, reproduce the issue, then export or save all requests as HAR.

  2. 02

    Follow the story

    Open the file or drop it anywhere. Filter requests, inspect timing phases, and look at Findings for clues. Add another HAR as a baseline to spot regressions.

  3. 03

    Share with intention

    Export a Markdown report or a sanitized HAR. Query values, credentials, cookies, bodies and custom fields are removed from HAR exports. URL paths and query names remain; review them before sharing. Sanitizing is not a guarantee that every secret is removed.
