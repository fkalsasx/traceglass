THE FIELD GUIDE

From “why is it slow?”
to “there it is.”

  1. 01

    Capture your network

    In Chrome, Edge or Firefox, open Developer Tools → Network. Reload your page, reproduce the issue, then export or save all requests as HAR.

  2. 02

    Follow the story

    Open a HAR or paste its JSON. Start with Overview, choose a page, and search with status:5xx time:>1s. Add a baseline to compare medians and new failures. Tune limits in Budgets & rules.

  3. 03

    Share with intention

    Export a printable HTML report, Markdown, CSV or a sanitized HAR. Exports follow the selected page, independently of the search box. Query values, credentials, cookies, bodies and custom fields are removed from HAR exports. URL paths and query names remain; review them before sharing. Sanitizing is not a guarantee that every secret is removed.
